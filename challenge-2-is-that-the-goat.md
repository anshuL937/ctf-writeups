# Challenge 2: Is that the GOAT...

**Category:** OSINT / Geolocation
**Goal:** From a vlog frame showing a turquoise lake with dead tree trunks standing in the water, identify the **national park** the lake is in (not the lake itself).
**Answer format:** `XXXXXX XXXXX XXXXXXXX XXXX` (6-5-8-4 letters)
**Flag:** `REDACTED`

## Approach

1. **Noted the distinctive features.** A "drowned forest" (tree trunks standing in turquoise water), pine-covered cliffs, and a YouTube *Subscribe* button showing the image was a frame from a travel vlog.

2. **Reverse image search.** Google Lens identified the lake as **Lake Kaindy**, known for its sunken forest, in the Tian Shan mountains of Kazakhstan.

3. **Verified with a proper source.** I didn't rely only on the AI summary. Wikipedia's Lake Kaindy article confirms the lake lies *within* a specific national park.

4. **Cross-checked from the park's side.** The park's own article lists Lake Kaindy as being inside it. The two articles spell the name differently because both are transliterations of the same Kazakh name.

5. **Matched the answer format.** Checked the park name against the required 6-5-8-4 letter pattern before submitting. Accepted.

## Lessons

- Read the question carefully. It asked for the **park**, not the lake.
- Confirm AI search answers against a primary source, from both directions where possible.
- Use the given answer format as a final check.
